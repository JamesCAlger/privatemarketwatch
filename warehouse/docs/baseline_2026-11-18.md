Observations (measured, not a finding of root cause beyond this check):

- 11 of the 74 fund-quarters with positions differ from the filed total by more than 0.5M. All 11 are Apollo Debt Solutions BDC, from 55.0M to 785.9M.
- In each of the 11, the difference equals (to 0.1M) the fair value of Apollo's published rows whose asset category is CASH. Apollo is the only one of the five funds with published CASH rows (18 rows across 13 quarters); the other four have none. So cash is treated differently between funds in the published holdings. Whether cash belongs in the published total is an open question for the owner.
- The reconciliation is not an independent proof. The pipeline's per-fund rules were tuned against the same filed total, so a tie is a proxy for correctness.
- AGENTS.md lists an HPS overshoot of about 6 pct at 2026-03-31 as an open defect. In this build HPS ties to the filed total in every quarter with positions (difference 0.0M). This was not investigated.
