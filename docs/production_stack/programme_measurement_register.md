## 1. Measurement populations and result contract

Use the following versioned sets for each required quarter `q`:

- `O_q`: all cohort fund-quarter obligations, with source-backed lifecycle eligibility. There are 70 x 13 = 910 required-period obligation records before eligibility. Missing filing, tagging or output is not evidence of non-operation.
- `H_q`: all final retained holdings for the cohort and quarter, across classifications and accounting record kinds, bound to one candidate release. This is the public headline population. Include zero, negative and unknown-classification records; report missing FV separately.
- `R_q`: independently established source position/lot roster on declared schedules and accounting populations. Explicit presentation duplicates/subtotals have evidence-backed exclusions. A roster generated with the same defective parser is insufficient independent acceptance evidence.
- `A_q`: independent fund-quarter source anchors with known currency, date and accounting scope, plus an explicit mapping from relevant holdings to each anchor. Use separate cash, parent-interest and underlying-schedule populations where needed.
- `E_fq`: observations eligible for field `f` based on instrument applicability and source disclosure/evidence. Keep absent source, extraction failure, unknown applicability and not applicable as separate counts. Eligibility cannot be inferred from whether the candidate populated the field.
- `G`: versioned independently adjudicated benchmark units: source schedules/fields, borrower links, position continuations/chains, enrichment decisions and citation passages. Sampling frame, selection probabilities where applicable, source versions, label versions and review status are retained.

Public totals use signed FV. Error/coverage weights use absolute FV, with `W(X) = sum(abs(FV))` over records whose FV is known and on a compatible currency basis. Report unknown-FV row counts and monetary assessability; missing FV must not quietly contribute zero to a claimed full-population coverage percentage. Precision FV weights use published FV; source recall weights use independent source FV. Do not use the candidate's missing/incorrect value as the source-recall weight.

Each result must store:

|---|---|
| Identity | `release_id`, `criterion_id`, `metric_id`, `metric_version`, `quarter`, scope/stratum ID, evaluation timestamp, evaluator code identity |
| Inputs | Cohort/schema/decision/rule versions, artifact content identities, benchmark and label versions, reference-source identities; local-change hash when the worktree is dirty |

## 2. Formulas and assessability rules

### Roster completeness and purity: A2

Establish a one-to-one correspondence between source-defined observations/lots and published rows within a declared comparable population. Legitimate source duplicates may map to one selected source observation only after documented source adjudication; matching multiple published duplicates to the same source row cannot increase recall or precision.

- Row recall = correctly represented source positions / all eligible source positions in the assessed roster.
- Row precision = supported published positions / all assessed published positions, including extras, subtotals and duplicates.
- FV recall = source-side absolute FV of correctly represented positions / source-side absolute FV of all eligible reference positions.
- FV precision = published-side absolute FV of supported positions / published-side absolute FV of all assessed published positions.

These are roster metrics; a correctly identified position with a wrong amount can pass roster matching and fail B2. State that distinction. Report under-inclusion and over-inclusion independently. Record assessed schedule/fund/quarter coverage and independent review coverage. Unassessed schedules remain unknown; do not extrapolate a convenience sample to the full cohort. Independent sampling and weights are required for a population estimate. Preserve zero/missing-FV rows in row measures.

### Field fidelity and availability: B2

For each field and applicable source-observation pair, compare candidate value/meaning/unit against independent normalized source evidence. A missing candidate for an eligible disclosed fact is an error; an unsupported populated value is an error in a separate published-value check. An undisclosed optional field is not an extraction failure.

Field accuracy = correct assessed eligible disclosures / all assessed eligible disclosures; compute both row-weighted and absolute-FV-weighted versions. Also report unsupported populated values / assessed populated values, disclosure coverage, extraction coverage conditional on disclosure, and evidence assessability. Keep source-copy fidelity separate from correctness of calculated/inferred/default/proxy values, which have their own formula/eligibility tests under B1/D1/D2.

Proposed comparison rule: `abs(actual - reference) <= max(source_rounding_bound, approved_abs_floor[f], approved_rel_tol[f] * abs(reference))`. The source rounding bound comes from declared decimals/scaling or displayed precision; inconsistent sources require review. Field-specific floors/tolerances remain pending calibration. Wrong currency, rate kind or denominator fails meaning checks even if a broad numeric tolerance happens to pass. Missing inputs do not become zero.

Exact contractual examples are independent of aggregate error tolerance: 11.49% disclosed cash plus 2% PIK gives 13.49% all-in; reported 13.49% all-in stays 13.49%; source zero cost remains zero; missing rates stay unavailable; an explicit USD unit resolves without FX. Preserve legitimate zero/negative values and counterexamples.

### Fund reconciliation and public consistency: B3/B4

For each independent anchor `a`, let `P_a` be the holdings sum on the declared comparable accounting basis and `T_a` the source total. Store signed residual `d_a=P_a-T_a`, absolute residual `abs(d_a)`, percentage `abs(d_a)/abs(T_a)` where defined, and the basis bridge from published holdings. A zero anchor uses an approved absolute-dollar rule; percentage remains undefined.

For the cohort reconciliation headline, select one declared primary comparable investment-total anchor per assessed fund-quarter. Aggregate discrepancy = `sum(abs(d_a)) / sum(abs(T_a))` across those primary anchors; never net positive and negative errors. Supplemental cash or subsidiary schedules are evaluated separately rather than double-counting overlapping anchors in the aggregate denominator. Report fund-count and FV assessability plus missing/ambiguous anchors.

B4 is a separate exact invariant: canonical signed `sum(H_q.fair_value)` equals the unrounded headline, instrument-bucket total and sector-bucket total under the same serialization/aggregation convention. Each row has exactly one bucket per partition, including unknown. Check contributor IDs, not just equal sums, because errors can cancel. No overlays, scaling, matched-only populations or positive-FV-only filters for these totals. Missing/foreign-unresolved values remain a separately measured data-quality limitation rather than an invented amount.

### Identity, continuation and chain metrics: C2/C3

Entity link precision = correct accepted same-company links / adjudicable accepted links. Recall = recovered same-company links / all adjudicated true same-company links in the independent frame, including missed candidates. The frame must include within-filer, cross-filer and temporal cases, aliases, unrelated same-name borrowers and parent/subsidiary distinctions. Accepted-link review alone cannot measure recall; name fill rate cannot measure either metric. Do not let a single large cluster generate enough easy pair combinations to hide false mergers: publish by entity/cluster and stratum as well as sampled pair results.

For position continuation, use the existing source-question outcome vocabulary:

- Precision = `correct_link / (correct_link + wrong_link + false_link)`.
- Recall = `correct_link / (correct_link + wrong_link + missing_link)`.
- End accuracy = `correct_end / (correct_end + false_link)` on evidenced ends.
- Complete-chain rate = reference chains whose expected member observations and links are all present, with no extra merged members / all fully adjudicated eligible reference chains. Report both unweighted and an explicitly declared FV weighting; repeated quarters must not silently multiply a chain's weight. Default design: mean absolute source FV across its observed quarters.

Wrong continuation can contribute both a false proposed link and a missed true link; it must not disappear from recall. Evaluate adjacent-quarter and gap-spanning continuations separately. A filed position's disappearance alone is not adjudicated proof of sale. Report singletons separately from multi-quarter chains. Data-extraction omissions are visible in end-to-end chain coverage and separately attributed from matcher-only failures.

Uncertain/missing/invalid adjudications remain in benchmark coverage and unresolved counts. For accepted links, show decidable precision plus conservative bounds: confirmed / all reviewed proposed links as lower bound, and (confirmed + unresolved) / all reviewed proposed links as upper bound, where those categories exhaust the reviewed frame. These bounds are not confidence intervals. A zero denominator is `UNMEASURED`/not applicable with evidence, never perfect accuracy. Held-out assessability floors must be calibrated before pass/fail use.

### Enrichment and citations: D3-D5

GICS sector accuracy = correct sector assignments / adjudicable published assignments in an independently reviewed, level-specific benchmark. Coverage = assigned rows / in-scope holdings rows and assigned absolute FV / known absolute FV, with unknown-FV counts separately. Also report assignment-level results, because repeating one company decision across many holdings is not independent evidence. Stratify by rule/model/source mapping/default pathway and exposure. Optional finer-level assignments get their own result if published; no mandatory sub-industry completion gate.

Citation referential validity = links resolving to the correct pinned document/locator / all released citation links. Source-highlight coverage = values with a valid supporting exact passage / all eligible reported-value displays, by display type and row/FV strata where meaningful. Calculated and inferred displays are scored separately for a complete derivation/evidence path, not falsely required to have one reported source number. Every numerical display has either that path or an explicit unavailable reason. A working URL is not semantic citation correctness; independently check the highlighted passage actually supports the claimed field/meaning. Repeated identical values in the wrong row are failures.

### Sampling and pass rules

Record sample size, selection design, strata and clustering by fund, company/decision and chain as appropriate. Existing Wilson intervals are reusable for appropriate unweighted binomial samples; they do not automatically justify uncertainty for exposure-weighted or clustered estimates. For designed samples use the known selection weights and a declared stratified/cluster-aware interval method; describe judgment samples as benchmark results only.

Before remediation, freeze benchmark splits, assessability floors, minimum sample/cluster sizes and whether a target applies to a point estimate or a confidence bound. This register does not silently convert the proposed 99.5% precision into a statistically certified 99.5% population guarantee. Include apparently passing schedules and the long tail alongside high-FV/error-prone cases. Review labels independently; prior model confidence or worker adjudication alone is insufficient acceptance truth. Preserve label revisions and contamination history.

Owner direction, 10 September: use automated source verification without a mandatory human-labelled gold set or human review sample. Apply full-population deterministic comparisons and designed source-selected automated audits, including apparently passing schedules. Keep probability-sample estimates separate from targeted diagnostic results. Units must match the claim: complete rosters for omissions, distinct entity/sector decisions and position chains. Specify sample-size/cluster calculations and time/compute limits. Automated reference admission must verify the evidence and meaning required by the particular metric; model agreement and shared-parser consistency alone do not certify accuracy. Sampling intervals do not account for systematic reference errors. See [automated reference verification](programme_spec_status.md#automated-reference-verification-no-human-label-prerequisite). No numerical target or confidence rule changes here.

### 12.2 What a verification packet contains

The separate document path must enumerate the whole schedule, not only facts returned by a whitelist or rows named in a worker packet. An automated challenger must inspect the original row, headers, notes, alternative interpretation and exclusions independently of the correction's diagnosis. It can invalidate support; an agreement score cannot replace a missing predicate. Unsupported layouts or interpretations remain unresolved after bounded investigation. Measure parser/layout strata and report reference-dependency risk alongside results; do not relabel automated agreement as independently established population accuracy.

### 12.4 Position matching and roster scoring

Compare each admitted source roster with **all** candidate rows in the same fund, observation date and declared accounting population. Candidate extras remain in the precision denominator. Source omissions remain in the recall denominator.

1. Match through verified document/context/row lineage where available, then independently checked issuer, instrument/facility/lot terms and scope. Distinct tranches remain distinct. Names, equal amounts and a production PID alone are insufficient.
2. Establish a one-to-one assignment. For repeated source presentations, first resolve the source relationship under AV-03; preserve their evidence. Two candidate copies cannot both claim the same source position. Never break an ambiguous tie just to maximize the score.
3. Record `matched`, `missing_output`, `unsupported_output`, `duplicate_output` or `unresolved_mapping`. Unmatched does not automatically mean unsupported: establish whether the reference covers the relevant source scope before assigning an error.
4. Keep roster matching separate from field scoring. A wrong FV does not make a source-identified loan disappear from the roster. Do not use agreement on the tested value as the sole evidence that the row is correctly matched.

Apply section 2's row/FV recall and precision formulas. Recall uses independent source absolute FV; precision uses candidate absolute FV. Signed totals remain separate. A matching position with an incorrect amount may pass roster membership and fail field fidelity. Report duplicates and omissions individually even when their amounts cancel.

For a known roster with unresolved mappings, retain the entire denominator and report lower/upper correctness bounds: confirmed correct / eligible, and (confirmed correct + unresolved) / eligible. These are ambiguity bounds, not sampling confidence intervals. A nonzero unresolved pool cannot be dropped to create a whole-roster PASS under this draft. Known errors can still establish FAIL if even the upper bound misses the target. Unknown roster size or FV prevents the corresponding population claim; report assessed-subset results explicitly. Zero/missing-FV positions still contribute to row results.

### 12.5 Field scoring and missingness

Determine eligibility from the source and field contract before inspecting whether the candidate populated the value. Score all eligible disclosed fields, including positions entirely missing from output. Matched-row-only scores may be diagnostics but cannot supply end-to-end field accuracy.

| Source/candidate condition | Proposed scoring treatment |
|---|---|
| Disclosed, applicable, unambiguous value | Compare value **and** meaning against AV-05. Correct/mismatch/missing candidate is an explicit field outcome. An absent output row makes its supported disclosures missing, not ineligible. |
| Confirmed absent disclosure or inapplicable field | Exclude from disclosed-field accuracy with that reason. Evaluate populated candidate values under their declared derivation/proxy/default policy; unsupported population remains an error. |
| Unreadable source, ambiguous applicability or conflicting value | Retain an unresolved eligibility/reference count. Do not call it an absent disclosure or a correct null. Limit the affected metric's assessability. |
| Derived, default or proxy value | Check eligible inputs, permitted formula/default, evidence category and lineage separately. Correct arithmetic does not turn a proxy into a reported fact. |
| Source zero, negative amount, dash or nil | Preserve distinct states. Zero/negative values are not removed. A dash/nil becomes zero only when the source convention for that field establishes zero. |

Score FV, cost and principal separately against the existing 99.5% working requirement, and other numeric/date fields separately against 99%, by rows and applicable absolute-FV exposure. Do not pool strong FV results with weak rate results. **Draft weighting convention (AS-07):** field fidelity uses source FV so a candidate's bad FV cannot set its own importance; unsupported populated-value exposure uses candidate FV. Record unresolved weights rather than assigning zero. This makes section 2's field-weighting basis explicit for review.

Meaning checks precede numerical comparison:

- Resolve currency separately for principal, cost and FV. A unit ID is resolved within its document; contrary currency evidence prevents the USD default. A reported USD equivalent and an analytical FX conversion have different evidence paths.
- Distinguish cash coupon, PIK terms, all-in coupon, spread and benchmark. Apply the accepted all-in/cash/PIK contract and migrate storage and income consumers together. Missing rates remain unavailable; reported sufficient terms can support a labeled calculation.
- Keep NAV percentages and portfolio weights separate. Derive only from the correct declared denominator and date. A numerically similar percentage with the wrong denominator fails meaning.
- Preserve exact maturity dates and partial-date precision. Apply the accepted month-end convention for a disclosed month/year, retaining that precision; a missing date remains missing.
- Reported zero cost remains zero. Missing cost may use the accepted first-observed-FV proxy only with the separately verified donor/identity conditions; this procedure does not certify that donor chain.

Use decimal arithmetic and the evidence-backed source precision. For a rounded value, retain the permitted interval and its derivation (for example, half the displayed rounding unit where round-to-nearest is established). Unknown precision stays unresolved. No extra dollar floor, blanket 1% allowance or candidate-derived rounding is added. When tagged and displayed precision differ, document their consistency and the selected reference precision; do not choose the coarser representation to excuse an otherwise evidenced mismatch. Keep raw residuals even for comparisons within source precision.

Publish per-field correct/mismatch/missing/unsupported counts, source disclosure coverage, extraction coverage conditional on disclosure, unresolved eligibility/reference counts and monetary assessability. Unsupported populated-value rates remain explicit: this draft introduces no new numerical tolerance for them, and accepted exact prohibitions such as unsupported rate inference still fail exactly.

Keep primary and supplemental cash/subsidiary populations separate. Relevant subsidiary records remain in holdings; anchor-specific exclusion does not authorize a global drop or a public denominator change.

Unknown source denominators prevent the corresponding monetary claim. For a zero anchor show equality/residual and an undefined percentage; do not invent the still-unspecified nonzero absolute-dollar allowance. Acceptance needing that allowance remains unresolved for a versioned treatment.

The result key must include candidate, metric/reference versions, field where relevant, quarter, fund/scope and weighting. Produce the required per-quarter and per-fund results before overall aggregation. Implement coupled boundaries as one candidate package: schema/prompt producer, parser/validator, reference adapter, executor and downstream scorer/consumer agree on the same contract version.
