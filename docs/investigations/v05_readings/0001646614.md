## 1. Headline

- Fair value is within 1.5M of filed in every quarter, but 8 of 12 quarters get there through a
  promoted rule that removes one of every two rows with the same fair value and keeps "the first"
  (`02_historical_exact_fv_duplicates.json`). The row kept is often the roll-forward note row or a
  category subtotal, not the schedule row. Fair value ties; cost, name, class, rate, maturity and
  percent of net assets on those rows are wrong or missing. Cost is off by 5M to 27M in 8 quarters.
- One predicate on the raw tagged rows reproduces the filed fair value exactly in all 12 quarters and
  the filed cost exactly in 8 (d3.sql): row carries a cost tag or a percent tag AND its member text
  carries 'Original Acquisition Date' or 'Maturity Date', excluding 'Total', money market and swap rows.
- The four quarters that differ on fair value are all dollar values tagged with a foreign unit and
  converted a second time.

Tests of `source_encodings.schedule_row_tags` on the raw rows (b3.sql):
- `cost_or_percent_unrounded` (no cost tag and no percent tag): removes 7, 11, 11, 10, 10, 10, 13, 13,
  12, 12, 14, 12 contexts per quarter (81.8M to 135.3M; 48.7M to 52.1M from 2025-06-30). Every one is a
  note row or a "Fund's controlled investments ..." / "Fund's affiliated/non-controlled investments ..."
  row. No schedule row is removed (the leaf set that ties to filed needs cost or percent on every row).
  No value fact of this filer is rounded more coarsely than the unit (0 facts with decimals below -3).
- `quantity_or_percent`: same rows through 2025-09-30, but only 5 of 14 at 2025-12-31 and 4 of 12 at
  2026-03-31, because the "Fund's ... Portfolio Company" rows carry a principal or share tag there. Wrong
  test for this filer.
- `not_commitment_only`: not applicable (no commitment tag on these).
The sibling-context trap of `quantity_or_percent` does not arise under `cost_or_percent_unrounded`.

Fix: (1) wrapper `source_encodings.schedule_row_tags: ["cost_or_percent_unrounded"]`; (2) retire
`02_historical_exact_fv_duplicates.json` (dedup on report_date + fair_value, keep first: a plug that can
also merge two real positions of equal value) and `01_historical_axis_subtotals.json` (five pinned
quarters, a member list); `exclude_portfolio_company_axis_duplicates.json` becomes redundant; (3) make
the section subtotals non-positions by the wrapper's own leaf marker ('Original Acquisition Date' /
'Maturity Date' / 'Instrument' in the member), which is what ties in all 12 quarters. The wrapper's
`category_marker_re` only lists "<category> <country>" order; the filer also writes "<country>
<category>" ("Japan Senior Secured Bonds", "Senior Secured Debt Germany").
