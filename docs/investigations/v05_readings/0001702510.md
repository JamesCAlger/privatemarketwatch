## 5. Proposed fixes

- F1. Wrong-instant rows. For filing 0001702510-25-000023 move the contexts whose member starts
  "Structured Credit Investments, " from 2023-12-31 to 2024-12-31 (23 rows). A generic guard is
  possible (NOT tested as code; it fits these 23 rows and the two Trinity cases in the same
  batch): a row tagged on another instant whose member has no row at the report date, and, when
  that instant is the comparative date, is absent from the prior period's own filing.
- F2. Position test by facts, as a wrapper setting and before any duplicate handling: a context on
  the identifier axis is a position only if it carries a cost, principal, shares or percent fact.
  Replaces both promoted rules; holds at 2025-09-30 (keeps c-274, c-275), 2025-12-31 (drops c-383, c-386,
  c-389), 2026-03-31 (drops the five).
- F3. Rate cells for this filer: (i) spread tag with printed reference FIXED, or on an equity-table
  row, is the fixed / all-in rate: publish it as interest_rate, coupon Fixed, no reference, no
  spread; (ii) InvestmentInterestRatePaidInKind = 1 means the whole coupon is paid in kind: pik_rate
  = interest_rate.
- F4. `reference_rate_columns` from the printed "Reference Rate (2)" cell; codes SOFR, LIBOR, EURIBOR,
  SONIA, CORRA, CDOR, FIXED. Retire the date guess for this filer.
- F5. `source_encodings.foreign_unit_values: ["reported_in_usd"]` for cost (Project Carbo; the schedule
  cost column is dollars), or a one-row correction.
- F6. Names before 2025-06-30: accept members without the "Investment | (Non-)Affiliated Issuer" lead:
  "<section>, <issuer>, <industry>[ n]" with section in (First Lien Debt, Second Lien, Second Lien Debt,
  Equity Investments, Structured Credit Investments, Investment Funds).
- F7. Class: section "Structured Credit Investment(s)" wins over the name test "Fund".
