### B. Equal-valued twin rows dropped (REAL, GENERIC)

The filer tags repeated rows of one issuer with members ending "One", "Two", "Three" (also "- Two", lowercase "two").
When a twin has the same par and fair value as its sibling it is dropped as `excluded_affiliation_dedup`.
27 rows in 13 quarters (t16.sql): PlanSource Holdings 893 to 900 in every quarter; Coretrust -1 to -3 (4 quarters);
Kaseya -17 (2023-03-31); Airwavz Solutions 3,864 to 3,873 (2025-03-31 on); Smarsh -13 / -33 (2025-03-31 to 2025-12-31).
Printed (0000950170-25-067098): "PlanSource Holdings, Inc. Health Care Technology 9.83 % S + 5.50 % 12/30/26 905 901 900
(6) (8) PlanSource Holdings, Inc. Health Care Technology 9.83 % S + 5.50 % 12/30/26 905 901 900"; "Airwavz Solutions, Inc.
Wireless Telecommunication Services 10.19 % S + 5.75 % 03/31/27 3,912 3,867 3,864 (6) Airwavz Solutions, Inc. ...
3,912 3,867 3,864 (6) Airwavz ... 2,445 2,417 2,415". Dropped contexts: C_9883b6bf-a5b9-42b5-83ea-bd98a20b718a ("... Two"),
C_4c4d513a-9b24-4434-a03b-17496398f5f5 ("... One").
Fix: `staging.distinct_member_suffix_re`, case-insensitive: `(?i)\s(?:One|Two|Three|Four|Five|Six|Seven|Eight|Nine|Ten)$`
(the working-tree wrapper has no such key; the model's pattern is case-sensitive and would miss the 2024-06-30
"two"). Covers SRC_BDC01 (13) apart from the D and E rows.
