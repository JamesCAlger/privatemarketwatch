## 1. Headline

- Same layout as the sister fund (0001993402): a separate table "Investments-non-controlled/non-affiliated
  Commitment Type Commitment Expiration Date Unfunded Commitment (1) Fair Value (1)" after the schedule,
  tagged on the investment identifier axis. Here most of it is already kept out: 271 to 380 rows a
  quarter are removed by the promoted rule `exclude_commitment_fair_value_rows` or by the duplicate
  collapse. What still leaks is small: 1 to 4 commitment rows with a POSITIVE fair value a quarter
  (6 to 19 thousand), which the rule's predicate lets through.
- The BlackRock row is not a plug by value (each amount is the printed line) but it is hand-entered
  per quarter by two add rules, carries no accession, context or class, and will be missing again at
  the next quarter. The loader drops the real tagged row as a money market fund.
- Totals are within 19 thousand in every quarter. Raw schedule rows equal filed exactly in four
  quarters; in the last two they are 6 and 14 thousand below the printed total with cost exact; not
  located.
- instrument_description carries the whole tail of the label on 99 pct of rows.

### 3b. Test of `schedule_row_tags: ["cost_or_percent_unrounded"]`

Commitment contexts: no cost tag and no percent tag in all six quarters, so the setting removes all
of them (and before the duplicate collapse). It does not remove exactly those: in the first three
quarters some SCHEDULE rows have neither tag and carry a fair value: 8 rows -53 (2024-12-31), 8 rows -9
(2025-03-31), 6 rows +1 (2025-06-30). From 2025-09-30 every schedule row has a cost tag (837 + 9, 899,
896) and the setting is exact. Effect on totals against today: 2024-12-31 53 over instead of 6 over;
2025-03-31 9 over instead of 11; 2025-06-30 1 under instead of 19 over; later quarters lose the 7, 10
and 8 of leaked commitment rows. The exact predicate for this filer is the label:
`(?i)\bCommitment\s+Type\b|\bCommitment\s+Expiration\s+Date\b` with no further condition (the label test is
what the promoted rule uses; its extra condition `fair_value <= 0 OR cost = fair_value` is what lets the
positive rows through).
