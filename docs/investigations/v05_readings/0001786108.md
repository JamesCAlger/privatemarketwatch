# 0001786108 Trinity Capital Inc. - reading notes (sandbox l6b, 2026-10-10)

Read-only. Queries and helper scripts are in `scratch/2026-10-10_read/0001786108/` (c*.sql, dump.py,
census.py). Values in thousands of dollars unless marked M. "ctx" = XBRL context id.
Method: every printed schedule row (debt, warrant, equity) of all 13 quarters was parsed from the
cached filing and matched to published rows on cost and fair value (census.py). The parser misses
one or two equity rows in some quarters (2024-09-30 Nexii, 2024-12-31 and 2025-03-31 Busbot and
Centivo); those show as "published only" in its output and are printed.

## 1. Headline

- The seven fair-value gaps are five different causes, all found (section 2). Two of them are
  facts tagged on the wrong date, the same filing error as Carlyle 2024-12-31.
- Cost is short in ALL 13 quarters (-4.0M to -24.7M), which no finding reports. Two generic causes:
  rows printed with fair value "-" are dropped (28 to 77 rows per quarter, cost 10.5M to 30.4M) and a
  cost printed "-" is replaced by fair value (6 to 11 rows, +4.2M to +15.1M).
- interest_rate is the FLOOR of the rate formula on 80 to 107 Prime loans per quarter, not the rate
  in effect; it is null on loans with a PIK leg and on SOFR loans. The page prints formulas only.

### 4d. Rates (recurring defects a, c)

Printed column: "Interest Rate (4)"; footnote 4: "Interest rate is the fixed or variable rate of the debt
investments and does not include any original issue discount, end-of-term ...". The cell is a formula,
never a current all-in rate: "Fixed interest rate 12.5 %; EOT 4.0 %"; "Variable interest rate Prime + 4.0 %
or Floor rate 11.5 %; EOT 2.5 %"; "Variable interest rate SOFR 3 Month Term + 7.3 %; EOT 0.0 %"; "... Prime + 2.5 %

tagged (`interest_rate_source = xbrl_field` on the 74 fixed rows at 2026-03-31); the rate of a Prime

## 5. Proposed fixes

- F1. Wrong-instant facts (shared with Carlyle): within one filing, when a schedule member has a row
  at the report date with a cost and no fair value, and the same member has a fair value on another
  instant of that filing, take that fair value (2024-09-30, 10 rows, 3.5M); when a member has no row
  at the report date and exists only on an instant that is neither the report date nor the
  comparative date, move it to the report date (2026-03-31, 2 rows, 7.9M). Not tested as code.
- F2. `staging.distinct_member_suffix_re` cannot express it (the twins differ by a leading space
  character): add a switch that keeps two contexts as two positions when their raw members differ
  at all and each carries its own principal, cost and fair value. Restores 789 / 719 / 679.
- F3. Sub-total by percent tag: drop a context that carries a percent-of-net-assets fact when another
  context has the same cost and fair value (2024-03-31, 4,609).
- F4. Rate for this filer, from the member text: interest_rate = fixed rate; or max(reference + spread,
  floor) with the reference from a dated series (Prime, SOFR term, CORRA), marked as computed; plus the
  PIK leg under the all-in convention; never the EOT percentage. If a computed rate is not wanted,
  publish the floor in a separate field and leave interest_rate null rather than label a floor as
  the rate. Add reference codes CORRA and "Base Rate".
- F5. Issuer regex: make the industry and the hyphen optional, anchor the issuer as the text before
  "Type of Investment", allow the member to start mid-word ("rtfolio", "Securities-", "PortfolioPortfolio").
  Members with no company need the page (continuation rows).
- F6. Keep rows with a cost tag and no fair value as positions with fair value 0 (28 to 77 rows per
  quarter); do not fill a "-" cost from fair value (6 to 11 rows).
- One row each, filing wrong: Empower 2024-06-30 cost and principal (+10,000 each); NextCar second
  "April 18, 2022" loan at 2023-12-31 (add 3,006 / 3,006 / 2,528 from the page).
