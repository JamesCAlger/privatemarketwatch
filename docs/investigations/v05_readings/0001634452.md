- F1. Rows with a cost tag and no fair value tag on a leaf of this filer: publish with fair value 0
  (the page prints a dash) instead of dropping. 10 rows / 11.48M cost at 2025-12-31, 1 row 174,608 at

  warrants 2025-06-30). Generic: a fund whose filings print a dash should not receive
  `first_observed_fv_proxy`.
- F3. Wrapper `identifier_format.field_order` for the pipe form: section, lien, issuer_name, industry,
  facility type (-> instrument_description, with the lien), rate text, maturity, ordinal. Reference codes
  `{"S": "SOFR", "L": "LIBOR", "P": "PRIME"}`; "(S; n% PIK)" is floating SOFR with spread 0 and PIK n.
  From 2025-03-31 prefer the tagged enumeration.
- F4. `dispatch.issuer_name_strip_re` of 2e for the two member-per-position quarters.
- F5. 2023-03-31 and 2023-06-30 rates and maturities from the printed "Interest" and "Maturity" cells.
- F6. Fund-level wrapper: NII from "Net investment income after tax", TII from "Total investment income";
  a cost fact without an identifier member is not `investments_at_cost` when its value is below 1 pct of
  investments at fair value; shares from the balance sheet parenthetical.
- One row, filing wrong date: C_6fda8701 (2025-06-30, Medical Management Resource Group revolver,
  223,073). Generic form: in a 10-Q, an identifier context dated neither at the period end nor at the
  prior fiscal year end, whose identifier has no context at the period end, is the current row.
