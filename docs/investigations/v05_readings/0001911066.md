## 1. Headline

- Every tagged position row with a value is published in all 13 quarters; published non-cash rows equal the
  balance-sheet investments line exactly in every quarter read. The "5M to 16M under filed" in three 2023
  quarters is not missing positions: the filed figure used (`investments_at_fair_value`) is the schedule's
  "Total Investments", which in 2023 includes one un-itemised "Cash Equivalents (14)" line.
- The fund-level investments figure is on two different bases across quarters (with cash equivalents in 9
  quarters, without in 4). That one inconsistency produces all 10 fv_conservation, 5 gav_reconciliation,
  5 F20, 4 GAV_BDC02 and 1 GAV_BDC03 findings.

  2025-09-30 the filer tags them with its own concepts. 2025-09-30 is patched by a quarter-pinned row-add rule.
- `filed_rate_reading: all_in` is contradicted by the page: the plain rate tag holds the printed "(Cash)" figure.
- Issuer names are cut at the first comma in 11 quarters (to 2025-09-30); "LLC", "Inc. 1" become the instrument.
