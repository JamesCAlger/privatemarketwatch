Read-only. Queries and helper scripts are in `scratch/2026-10-10_read/0001901164/` (a1-b8 .sql, dump.py, refcount.py).
Values in thousands of dollars. "ctx" = XBRL context id.

## 1. Headline

- Totals: published equals the filed investments total to the dollar in all 12 quarters (2023-06-30 to
  2026-03-31). No tagged position row is unpublished (the only unmatched rows are the 154 rows of the
  original 10-Q 0001628280-23-028587, superseded by the 10-Q/A 0001628280-23-028716).
- What is wrong is everything the totals do not test: class (all equity and the asset-backed note are

  member text, ordinal included), foreign principal with no dollar value.

## 3. Real errors (none reported by a totals check)

### 3a. Loans with a PIK part publish no interest rate (REAL, GENERIC)

- Count: 15, 18, 13, 18, 21, 29, 35, 31, 44, 50 rows from 2023-12-31 to 2026-03-31 (`src_transforms` has
  `interest_rate:coupon_unresolved`); fair value 104.6M at 2023-12-31, 408.1M at 2026-03-31 (13.7 pct).
  These are the 10 `financial_convention_unresolved` findings and most of the 8 X02 findings.
- Printed: "NCWS Intermediate, Inc. (4) (5) S + 5.50 % ( 2.25 % PIK) 9.17 % 12/31/2029 61,243 60,822 57,721 3.52".
  Tagged (ctx c-39): InvestmentBasisSpreadVariableRate 0.0550, InvestmentInterestRatePaidInKind 0.0225,
  InvestmentInterestRate 0.0917. Published: interest_rate null, basis_spread 5.5, pik_rate 2.25
  (interest_rate_reported 9.17).
- The tagged rate is all-in and the tagged spread already contains the PIK part: same quarter "FleetPride,
  Inc. (4) (5) S + 5.50 % 9.17 %" (no PIK) prints the same 9.17. Measured over all quarters: of 225 floating
  rows with a PIK tag, rate minus spread equals the quarter's median (rate minus spread) of non-PIK dollar
  loans within 0.35 on 187; rate minus spread minus PIK fits on 5 (b5.sql).
- PIK-only fixed rows: "Associations, Inc. (4) (5) ( 14.25 % PIK) 14.25 % 5/3/2030 913 912 913 0.05" (ctx c-52):
  both InvestmentInterestRate and PaidInKind tagged 0.1425; published interest_rate null, pik_rate 14.25.
  13 such rows at 2026-03-31 (Cotiviti 7.63, Cheval Blanc 14.75, National Resilience 8.00, TC Signature x4,
  Associations x2, and the preferred rows Aptean 13.50, Eclipse Buyer 12.50, FleetPride 14.50, Iconic 5.00).
- Fix: wrapper `source_encodings.filed_rate_reading: ["all_in"]`. Then interest_rate = tagged rate,
  pik_rate = tagged PIK, and the spread is the total margin (cash plus PIK), which is what the page prints.
  Do NOT set `printed_margin_reading: cash_plus_pik` here: this page prints the total margin and the PIK part
  of it in brackets, not cash and PIK apart.

### 3e. Issuer name is the member text (REAL, GENERIC; D06 x2, C206, and "issuers per row about 1")

- All 12 quarters: `instrument_description` null on every row, distinct issuer_name = rows (384 of 384) while
  canonical_name has 143. 352 of 384 names end in an ordinal ("Packaging Coordinators Midco, Inc. 5").
- 2025-06-30 and 2025-09-30 (accessions 0001901164-25-000008, -000016): every name keeps the prefix
  "Investment, Unaffiliated Issuer, " (324 and 335 rows) and 12 / 9 carry lien or class text ("Investment,
  Unaffiliated Issuer, Polaris Newco LLC, Second Lien"; "..., Cheval Blanc Holdings Company, Debt Securities,
  First Lien"). One row at 2025-12-31 ("Investment, Unaffiliated Issuer, Kymora Ltd."). That is why D06 fails
  at 2025-06-30 (0 of 286 names persist) and 2025-12-31 (1 of 334).
- Fix: the wrapper's `canonical_strip_re` already removes the prefix for matching; apply the same strip, plus
  a trailing `\s+\d+$` (ordinal -> tranche number) and a trailing ", (First|Second) Lien|, Debt Securities, ...|,
  Common Stock|, Preferred (Equity|Stock)" (-> instrument_description), to issuer_name.
