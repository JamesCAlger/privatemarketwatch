## 2. Names: the CURRENT wrapper regexes tested on every distinct current-period identifier

Method: `0001508655/lg.py` loads the wrapper as `pipeline.staging_bdc` does, expands `(?:MSD_INDUSTRY_LABELS)`
(118 shared + 264 extra labels), and applies the guard condition, issuer and instrument extraction in DuckDB to
2,103 distinct current-period identifiers (2023-03-31 to 2026-03-31). Output `lg_fail.txt`, `lg_iss.txt`.

Still wrong under the current wrapper:

1. **'and Consumer Products ...' / 'and Leisure ...' issuers: about 290 published rows, every quarter (15 to 29 a quarter).**
   Cause: the shared labels contain 'Retail' and 'gaming'. 'Retail and Consumer Products' is not in the wrapper's
   own list, so only 'Retail' is stripped. For 'Hotel, Gaming and Leisure' the optional `(?:Hotel,\s+)?` eats
   'Hotel, ', then the shared label 'gaming' matches before the wrapper's own alternative can.
   Examples: 'and Consumer Products Tango Management Consulting, LLC', 'and Leisure Equinox Holdings, Inc.',
   'and Leisure IRGSE Holding Corp.' (46 rows). These are in l4c and remain with the current wrapper.
2. **CLO rows of 2025-09-30, 2025-12-31, 2026-03-31 (about 40 a quarter).** Two causes:
   (a) heading without an industry ('Other Investments X', 'Structured Credit Investments X') is not stripped,
   because the prefix regex requires heading + industry: issuer becomes 'Structured Credit Investments Apidos CLO,';
   (b) 33 of them a quarter are written 'Series 2020 -4A'. The code turns ' -' into ' - ' and the guard then
   refuses the row (`NOT contains(norm_raw, ' - ')`), so it falls to the dash split: published issuer
   'Structured Credit Investments CIFC Funding Ltd, Series 2020', instrument empty. Same for 7 Octagon rows
   2023-03-31 to 2024-03-31 written with an en dash ('Series 2018 [en dash] 18A'). (b) is code, not regex.
3. Heading typos: 'DebtInvestments Human Resource Support Services bswift, LLC' (2024-06-30, 44.8M),
   'Debt Investments Mach Natural Resource LP,' and 'Debt Investments Mach Resource,' (2023-12-31, no industry).
4. Byte-order mark before the instrument: 'Madcap Software, Inc. [U+FEFF]First-lien loan' (2024-06-30, 2024-09-30,
   31.9M): `\s+` does not match it, the guard does not fire. 'HireVue, Inc.[U+FEFF]' keeps it in the issuer (3 ids).
5. Instrument words missing from `marker_re`: 'Common Units' (Bayshore, 2023-03-31/06-30, 11.4M),
   'Earnout Interests' (Khoros, 2025-06-30 to 2026-03-31, 7.2-7.5M), 'Series A Preferred Sotck' (ReliaQuest
   2023-12-31), 'Series A Preferred Units/Stock' (issuer keeps 'Series A'), 'ABL FILO term loan', 'Roll Up DIP term
   loan', 'Super-Priority DIP term loan', 'First Out Term Loan' (issuer keeps 'ABL FILO' etc: USR Parent, Bed Bath and
   Beyond, 99 Cents Only, Project P, Hudson's Bay), 'N Series C Warrants' (IntelePeer).
6. Trailing punctuation splits one issuer into several: 'Avalara, Inc' / 'Avalara, Inc,' / 'Avalara, Inc.' /
   'Avalara, Inc.,'; 'Artisan Bidco, Inc .'; every pre-2025 CLO issuer ends in a comma ('Ares CLO Ltd,').
7. **Labels with no issuer at all (the member omits it): 16 identifiers.** 2023-09-30 and 2023-12-31
   'Debt Investments, Manufacturing, First-lien loan (GBP 16,640 par ...)', '(EUR 4,851 par ...)', 'Unsecured Note
   ($1,059 par ...)', 'Debt Investments, Transportation, First-lien revolving loan ($1,667 par ...)'; 2024-06-30 five
   Manufacturing rows (28.4M), Transportation revolver, Retail 'First-lien loan ($1,347 par, due 9/2026)' and
   'Subordinated note ($4,740 par, due 9/2026)'; 2024-12-31 'Hotel, Gaming and Leisure Second-lien loan ($2,347 par,
   due 6/2027)'. From the same terms in neighbouring quarters these look like Skylark UK DebtCo, ASP Unifrax,
   Marcura Equities, American Achievement and Equinox, but I did not read each on the page. No regex can supply
   the issuer; it needs the page (section bridge) or a match on instrument + par + maturity + acquisition date to
   the adjacent quarter.
8. Labels with no instrument: 'Rapid Data GmbH Unternehmensberatung' (2023-09-30, 4.63M, the FV half of a split
   row, see 3b), 'Marcura Equities LTD' (no fair value).

Proposed regex set, tested the same way: `0001508655/proposed_leaf_guard.json` (the file shows single
backslashes; double them for JSON). Changes: heading list gains 'Other Investments', 'Controlled Affiliated
Investments' and `Debt\s*Investments`; industry becomes optional and lists 'Retail and Consumer Products' and
'Hotel,? Gaming,? and Leisure' BEFORE the shared labels (the `(?:Hotel,\s+)?` group is removed); markers add
common units, earnout interests, structured credit, the ABL FILO / DIP / First Out forms, 'series x preferred
units|stock|sotck', 'N series x warrants'; trailing strip also removes trailing commas, ' .', and U+FEFF.
Result (`lg_iss_prop.txt`): 255 distinct issuers, no heading or industry left. Not fixed by regex: items 2(b), 4
(Madcap), 7, 8, and oddities 'Raptor US Buyer II Corp.1,364', 'KKR CLO Ltd, 49A'. The optional industry is a
risk for an issuer whose name starts with an industry word; none seen in this fund's 2,103 identifiers.
Code-side asks: let the guard accept ' -' inside 'Series NNNN -NA' (or a wrapper switch to skip the dash test),
and strip U+FEFF from the raw identifier.
