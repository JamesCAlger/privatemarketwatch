## 2. What differs between the two wrappers (0002052152 vs 0002052153)

1. `source_encodings.printed_margin_reading: ["cash_plus_pik"]` is in 0002052152 and 0001837532 and
   MISSING here. All other source_encodings keys are identical (principal_usd_reference fred_h10,
   filed_rate_reading all_in, pik_only_tag_reading coupon, foreign_unit_values reported_in_usd,
   reference_rate_columns "Interest Rate", reference_rate_codes C=CORRA, BBSW=BBSW). Neither has
   `cash_identifiers` (0001837532 has "US BANK GCTS0095").
2. This wrapper declares a "fund" family: `leaf_markers_by_family.fund: ["co-investment"]`, a fallback
   pattern `(?i)^Co-investment$` -> fund, and a "fund" archetype. So the footnote total is dispatched as a
   fund POSITION (recon disposition fund_position_leaf, status missing_from_pipeline: the 5
   blocking_source_short_plain_unresolved and 5 SRC_BDC01 findings, 382M to 528M) and then removed by the
   promoted rule. The sister has no such family. Fix: delete the fund family and add `Co-investment`
   to `category_marker_re`; retire the rule.
3. `canonical_strip_re`: the sister strips the rate clause (`\s+Interest\s+Rate\s+.*?(?=\s+Maturity\s+Date|$)`);
   this one strips the leading industry instead (a long alternation) and sets
   `category_marker_before_total: true`; `category_marker_re` here lists the industries, the sister's is
   `^(?:Investments\s+(?:after|before)\s+Cash\s+Equivalents|Investments|Total\s+.+)$`.
4. Leaf markers: this one adds "prime", "preferred stocks", `\b(?:S|E|L|P)\+\d` and `\bStock\b`; the
   industry list here is longer (41 vs 19 names).
Result is the same published shape (issuer = "Group Entity", instrument carries rate and maturity text).
