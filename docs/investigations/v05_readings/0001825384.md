## 2. Names (every quarter; rules C206, D06)

issuer_name is the whole member text and instrument_description is NULL on every row of all 13 quarters
(issuer_name = identifier on 139 of 139 ... 340 of 342). Distinct issuers = rows. The true issuer count, splitting
at the instrument word, is about half: 69 of 139 (2023-03-31), 114 of 237 (2024-12-31), 154 of 342 (2026-03-31).

Loan Due 8/31/2028" (no commas, 2023); "TA/WEG Intermediate Holdings, LLC, First Lien Revolving Loan, One, Due
10/4/2027"; "GT Polaris Holdings, Inc., Equity Investment" (to 2024-12-31) then "..., Preferred Equity, Acquisition Date
6/6/2022" (from 2025-03-31); bare "RSC Topco, Inc."; one raw id "none_cnst_string_TaWegIntermediateHoldingsLlcUnsecuredNoteDue5262033".
The wrapper's `identifier_format` (delimiter ", ") is not applied or cannot work because issuers contain ", Inc.".
Fix: split on the first instrument phrase, wrapper regex such as
`^(.*?)[,\s]+((?:First|Second) Lien [A-Za-z ]*?Loan|Unsecured Note|Senior Note|Preferred Equity|Common Equity|Equity(?: Investment)?|Warrants)\b[,\s]*(?:(One|Two|Three|Four|Five)[,\s]*)?(?:Due,?\s*|Acquisition Date\s*)?([\d/]+)?`.
Typos to tolerate: "term Loan", "Team Loan", "Delayed Darw".
D06 (2024-12-31, 2025-03-31; continuity 42 and 49 pct) is this defect: continuity is computed on issuer_name, and
the member wording changed with the filing agent at 2025-03-31. Not a portfolio break.
