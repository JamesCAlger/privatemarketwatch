# 0001959604 Jefferies Credit Partners BDC Inc. - reading notes (sandbox l9b, 2026-10-10)

Read-only. Ten quarters, 2023-12-31 to 2026-03-31. Queries in `scratch/2026-10-10_read/0001959604/`
(j1.sql to j10.sql, tally.py). Values in thousands of dollars. "ctx" = XBRL context id.

## 1. Headline

- One mechanism explains the totals gap in all ten quarters, to the thousand. The filing has a second
  table after the schedule, "Unfunded Portfolio Company Commitments" (columns: portfolio company,
  investment type, commitment expiration date, unfunded commitment, fair value). Its rows are tagged on
  the investment identifier axis with a fair value and are published as positions. Each one repeats
  the (negative) fair value already carried by the schedule row of the same undrawn line, so the
  fund is published under its filed total. In addition, where a commitment row and its schedule twin
  collide, the pipeline sometimes keeps the commitment row and drops the schedule row.
- Raw schedule rows alone (every tagged row that is not a commitment-table row and not a subtotal)
  equal the filed investments total EXACTLY in all ten quarters, fair value and cost (section 3a).
- The same rows explain every other screen figure: the 17 to 25 pct of loans with no maturity, the
  22 to 92 rows per quarter with no instrument and a whole-label issuer name, the X02 counts, the D06
  continuity failures and most of the cost gaps.
- "No interest rate on 100 pct of loans" is as filed: the page has no rate column.

## 5. Proposed fixes

- F1. Do not publish the commitment table. Tested predicates on the raw rows:
  (i) the existing `source_encodings.schedule_row_tags: ["cost_or_percent_unrounded"]`: removes every
  commitment row in all ten quarters (none has a cost or percent tag; no value fact is rounded past the
  thousand), and because it acts before the duplicate collapse the schedule twin is kept. It does NOT
  remove exactly those rows: it also removes the schedule rows whose cost is printed as a dash
  (this filer tags percent of net assets on subtotals only, so cost is the only anchor): 1 row -40
  (2024-09-30), 4 rows -57 (2025-03-31), 6 rows -98 and one +2 (2025-06-30), 1 row -36 (2025-12-31).
  Result: ties exactly in six quarters, short by 40, 57, 96 and 36 thousand in four. Under 0.1M.
  (ii) exact: a context is a commitment-table row when it carries
  us-gaap:InvestmentCompanyFinancialCommitmentToInvesteeFutureAmount and no cost tag. By the sums in 3a
  this gives filed in ten of ten. Not verified context by context that every commitment row carries
  the tag (fact counts are consistent; two contexts read).
  Either way add the label form to the wrapper as a guard: `^\s*Portfolio\s+Company\s+` with no
  'Reference Rate' and no 'Maturity Date', and in 10-Ks '<issuer> Investment Type <type>' with no prefix.
- F2. Publish schedule rows that have a cost tag and no fair value tag with fair value 0 (77 rows,
  cost -9 to -219 a quarter). Then published cost equals filed cost.
- F3. `category_marker_re`: also match '... Equity Investments (Common Stock|Preferred Stock|L.P.
  Interests?) <industry>' with nothing after the industry (two rows, 1,161, 2025-03-31).
- F4. Reference rate from the tag where filed (2025-03-31 on) or from the printed code:
  `reference_rate_codes` S, E, C, SN, T, P.
- F5. `pik_only_tag_reading` for the fixed PIK loan (KL Stockton).
- F6. `principal_usd_reference: ["fred_h10"]`.
- F7. Fund-level: net assets every quarter; NAV per share 2025-12-31 (14.43) and 2026-03-31 (14.38);
  shares 2026-03-31 (56,735,669). No fund highlights wrapper exists.
- Names: the two regexes in 3d.
