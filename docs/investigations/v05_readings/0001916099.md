# 0001916099 Diameter Credit Co - reading notes (sandbox l6b, 2026-10-10)

Read-only. Queries and helper scripts are in `scratch/2026-10-10_read/0001916099/` (a*.sql, dump.py,
census2.py). Values in thousands of dollars unless marked M. "ctx" = XBRL context id.
Method: every printed schedule row of all 9 quarters was parsed from the cached filing (one table
row per line, `<acc>.txt`) and matched to the published rows on fair value and cost (census2.py).
The parser reproduces the printed totals exactly in 8 of 9 quarters (2024-09-30 stops at an earlier
"Total Investments" line and misses the two preferred rows; ignore that quarter's row counts).

## 1. Headline

- Fair value ties to the filed total in all 9 quarters. Cost does not: published minus filed is
  +13, +46, +53, +207, +471, +505, +734, -52,121, +779 (2024-03-31 to 2026-03-31). Three causes, all found.
- Rates, spreads, PIK, reference rate and maturity equal the page on every funded loan row in all
  9 quarters except the items in section 3d. The "26 to 29 pct of loans with no rate" in the cohort
  screen is as filed: every such row is an undrawn commitment (principal "-"), and the page prints
  no rate on those rows.
- Names are wrong on every row (section 3c).

## 4. Proposed fixes

- F1. `staging.hierarchy_prefix_re`: also accept "Other Equity" without the leading "Equity", and
  "Equity Preferred Equity"; keep the industry out of issuer_name.
- F2. Split issuer from instrument at the first instrument marker (Term Loan, Delayed Draw Term Loan,
  Revolving Line of Credit, Corporate Bond, Common Units / Shares, Class ... Units, Series ... Preferred
  Shares / Stock, Preferred Units, Warrants, Limited Partner Interest), strip a " - " joiner and
  "( n units)"; treat "Acquisition Date" / "Maturity Date" on an equity-family member as the acquisition
  date and publish no maturity for equity.
- F3. Wrapper switch to keep a schedule row that has a cost tag and no fair value fact (or a nil
  one) as a position with fair value 0. Measured: 2 to 17 rows per quarter, cost -13 to -771.
- F4. `reference_rate_codes`: P = PRIME, STIBOR; read "Dividend Rate n%" like "Interest Rate n%"; read the
  spread on preferred rows.
- F5. Extend the Action Elevator rule to cost (factor -1 on cost, same predicate).
- F6. Fund-highlights wrapper: distribution_per_share from the filer's
  InvestmentCompanyDistributionsDeclaredPerShare, not from the preferred-dividend line.
- F7. Do not fill cost from fair value when the cost cell is printed "-" (a tagged zero or nil).
- One row, filing wrong: Constellation second context 1,249 dollars (exclude a context whose label
  is a prefix of another context's label for the same issuer and whose value is 1/1000 of it).
