## 1. Headline

- The whole "over" is one thing: the schedule lists every revolver / delayed draw row at the full
  commitment (par and value include the undrawn part, rows marked "(C)") and then prints one line
  "Less unfunded loan commitments". The filed investments figure is the NET line. The line is tagged
  on the investment axis ("Short-term investments x% | Less unfunded loan commitments (y%)") and is
  dropped by the wrapper (`aggregate_markers` / non_private). Bridged to the dollar in all 9 quarters (2).
- The filed figure also INCLUDES the Dreyfus money fund. It is published as CASH to 2025-03-31 and
  dropped from 2025-06-30 (the filer moved it under the "Senior loans x%" prefix).
- 2025-12-31 ties only because a quarter-pinned promoted rule adds four rows by hand (3f).
- Cost is invented on every row of every quarter (cost = fair value) by a promoted rule (3f).
- Net assets missing in fund_financials for all quarters (3d).

## 3. Real errors

### 3a. Undrawn commitments published gross (17.5M to 105.6M, all quarters) - REAL, GENERIC (cause 11)

Printed, 0001193125-25-178880: "Steward Partners Global Advisory LLC, Revolver (C) - 10-13-28 955,881 946,322"
(no rate: wholly undrawn; published fair value 946,322, interest_rate null). The commitments note tags
each issuer's undrawn amount: `us-gaap:OtherCommitment` on `dei:LegalEntityAxis` (issuer member) x
`us-gaap:CreditFacilityAxis` (RevolverMember / DelayedDrawTermLoanMember) x
`OtherCommitmentsAxis=UnfundedDebtAndEquityCommitmentsMember`, e.g. ctx
`PAsOn03_31_2026_FourMCapitalLtdMemberdeiLegalEntityAxis_RevolverMemberusgaapCreditFacilityAxis_UnfundedDebtAndEquityCommitmentsMemberusgaapOtherCommitmentsAxis`
= 528,000. 149 such facts at 2026-03-31 sum 205,728,184 = 2 x 102,864,092 (each amount is tagged on
the facility and again on the issuer total: so the facility-level facts sum to the printed line).
`InvestmentCompanyFinancialCommitmentToInvesteeFutureAmount` is not used (0 facts in all 9 filings),
so `unfunded_rows: ["net_of_tagged_commitment"]` as built for KKR does not fire here.

Fix options (owner ruled "net each loan" for KKR, D-091):
1. Extend `source_encodings.unfunded_rows` with a second reading, e.g. `net_of_other_commitment`,
   that nets `OtherCommitment` (issuer x facility type) from that issuer's Revolver / Delayed Draw
   row. Needs a join issuer member -> schedule row by name and facility word; issuers with two
   delayed-draw rows need a rule (not tested here).
2. Cheaper and exact on the total: publish the tagged "Less unfunded loan commitments" line as one
   negative row (what the pinned rule does for 2025-12-31). Ties every quarter, but it is not a
   position and the per-row values stay gross.
Either way remove "less unfunded loan commitments" from `aggregate_markers` only if option 2.
Not tested: whether wholly undrawn rows equal their commitment exactly (rows with rate 0 sum 58.9M at
2026-03-31 against 102.9M undrawn, so partly drawn rows carry the rest).
