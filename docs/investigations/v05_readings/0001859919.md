## 5. Proposed fixes (generic, wrapper-switched)

- F1. Staging switch for this filer: a context on the identifier axis is a position only if it carries
  a cost tag or a percent-of-net-assets tag, and no value fact at decimals coarser than -3. Apply
  BEFORE the duplicate collapse. Retire the four promoted rules (two of them are keyed to context ids
  or one value). Measured: raw rows under this predicate equal filed in 13 of 13 quarters; fixes
  2023-06-30 (+203.4M), restores Core Scientific (26), removes the extra Rocade row (315), and makes
  the schedule twin the published one (cost gaps of 6.0M to 6.6M in four quarters and 31.1M at 2026-03-31).
  Needs the tag presence and decimals carried to staging (src_facts already has `_financial_cost.state`).
- F2. Do not treat a bare instrument name as a category row when the context carries principal, cost
  and percent (c-329 2,388; c-86 350). The wrapper's `category_marker_re` and `aggregate_markers` list
  bare "First Lien Senior Secured Term Loan" / "Subordinated Term Loan". Covered by F1's test if the
  aggregate rule is made to yield to it. Issuer for these two rows comes only from the page.
- F3. Reference rate from the printed rate cell (as maturity already is), not from the date; codes
  needed: SOFR, LIBOR, EURIBOR, SONIA, BBSY, CORRA, CDOR, BKBM, SARON, STIBOR, NIBOR, PRIME.
- F4. PIK-only loans: set interest_rate to the PIK rate under the all-in reading (or record that the
  field is cash-only for this filer; the wrapper says all_in).
- F5. Join a rate-only context to its numbered sibling ("X Term Loan" rates onto "X Term Loan 1" values)
  when the issuer has exactly one valued row lacking rates, or read the rate from the printed row.
- F6. Older member format: split "issuer, [industry,] instrument [n]" on the last segment that matches
  an instrument marker; keep industry out of issuer_name and out of the class test (removes the 7
  DIRECT_REAL_ESTATE rows).
- F7. Maturity on numbered-tranche rows in the pre-2024-12-31 layout (30 to 40 pct of loans missing).
- One row, filing wrong: Coastal Marina 8.0 pct cash loan tagged as PIK.
