## B. Proposed parsing (exact values are in the JSON block further down and in proposal.json)

B.1 Shared `_DOUBLE_INVESTMENTS_HIERARCHY_RE` (pipeline/staging_bdc.py ~938). It is applied only to wrappers that declare the prefix
'Investments Investments' and do not use prefix_strip: today exactly these two Fidelity funds. Changes: optional comma after the first
word; first word optional (single 'Investments -- ...' form from 2025-06-30); truncated first words ('nvestments', 'vestments',
'InvesInvestments', '. Investments'); em dash and en dash and comma as separator; 'non-controlled / affiliate(d)' and the
no-slash form; optional comma before and after the section word. In Python source write the dashes as \u2014 \u2013; the JSON
below uses the RE2 form \x{2014}.

B.2 Wrapper `staging.leaf_guard`: `type_industry_prefix_re` strips (optionally) a repeated prefix (4 rows at 2024-09-30 of
0001899996 carry the prefix twice), the section word (First/Second Lien Debt, Unsecured Debt, Debt, Equity, Asset-Backed Securities,
Preferred Securities, the mutual fund section words) and the industry, each optional, commas tolerated. `marker_re` splits at
'Type <word>' or the first instrument word; `issuer_trailing_strip_re` removes a trailing comma, 'Type', 'One'..'Ten',
' - 4.17%' (money market yield) and '(i)(q)' footnote marks. `evidence_re` needs the instrument words too (unfunded rows such as
'Dynamic Connections, Ltd Delayed Draw Term Loan' carry no rate, date or unit word and fail today's evidence test).

B.3 Shared code, one line, switched by the wrapper (`leaf_guard.allow_dash: true`): the guard refuses any label that still holds
' - ' after dash normalisation. With the prefix stripped that still hits 2 to 12 rows a quarter (up to 30.4M): issuer
'Cadence - Southwick, Inc.' (31 rows over the quarters), blank rate 'Reference Rate and Spread - Interest Rate -' (16 rows),
'Money Market Fund - 4.17%' (7), and labels with the prefix twice. Those rows then go to the dash splitter and publish
'Debt <Industry> ...' or a fragment as the issuer. Also strip U+FEFF / U+200B from the identifier before parsing (shared; the
dispatch layer already does): 'Advertising[FEFF] MMGY Global LLC' (2025-06-30, 8.0M) otherwise keeps its industry.

B.4 Markerless positions (shared code, switched by the wrapper): equity rows of the comma form have no instrument word
('..., Equity, Investment Companies, Air Control Concepts Holdings, L.P'). The leaf guard cannot split them. Proposed key
`leaf_guard.bare_issuer_instrument: "Equity"` with the dispatch `leaf_marker_re` below as the gate: when the wrapper calls the row
a leaf and no marker matches, issuer = stripped label, instrument = the configured word. 2 rows here, 23 rows in 0001899996.

B.5 Dispatch (wrapper `dispatch`): add prefix rules for the comma and single forms and a fallback for truncated prefixes, a
`leaf_marker_re` for equity rows, and a `category_marker_re` that tolerates the affiliation remainder and commas (so comma-form
heading rows stay aggregates). Tested through `classify_identifier` with the proposed wrapper copy
