## 1. Headline

- Fair value ties in all four quarters, and the raw schedule rows equal filed exactly (335,387;
  978,839; 1,488,856; 1,694,770) and filed cost exactly.
- The tie depends on a promoted rule that is a label test, and on a wrapper setting that is only half
  true for this filer. The filing has a footnote table (18) giving cost and fair value of the
  non-dollar loans IN LOCAL CURRENCY, tagged on the identifier axis with foreign units. Those are true
  foreign amounts. The schedule rows of the same loans are dollars, some of them also tagged with a
  foreign unit. `foreign_unit_values: ["reported_in_usd"]` is right for the schedule rows and wrong for
  the footnote rows; the footnote rows are kept out only by `exclude_short_investment_type_axis_rows`.
- First quarter (2025-06-30, seed stage): net assets 1,000 times too large in fund_financials and
  percent of net assets 100 times too small on every row.
- Reference rate missing on the EURIBOR and SONIA loans (57.3M at 2026-03-31) and SOFR on the CORRA loans
  (23.0M).

predicate `bdc_dimensions_raw ILIKE '% Investment Type %'`, all quarters. It removes exactly the

wrapper itself anticipates schedule labels of the form '... Investment Type First Lien' (leaf marker
"investment type first lien", alternate delimiter " Investment Type "); the day the filer writes that
form in the schedule the rule deletes the schedule. `data/overrides/agent_b2_corrections/0002012139/`
exists and is empty.

a cost tag, nothing rounded past the thousand). It would instead remove schedule rows with a fair
value and no cost tag: 1 row -16 (2025-09-30; a second, -39, has a percent tag and is kept), 2 rows -44
(2025-12-31), 3 rows -34 (2026-03-31). Not suitable here.
Exact predicate for this filer: a context whose cost and fair value facts are in a non-USD unit and
which has no principal, percent, rate or maturity fact is a local-currency note row. On the raw rows
that selects 0, 6, 8, 8 = the footnote rows and none of the seven schedule rows listed above (each of
