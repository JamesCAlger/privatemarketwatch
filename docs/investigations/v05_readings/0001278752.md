## 1. Headline

- Every totals gap in all 13 quarters is explained to the dollar by five causes (section 2). Two are
  generic and wrapper-fixable (the bare "Warrants" category line published as a position; dollar values
  tagged in a foreign unit and converted again), two are single tag errors in the filing (a group subtotal
  tagged on a position member; a fair value tagged with the cost concept), one is a real position dropped
  because its member has no instrument word. One dollar at 2023-06-30 is page rounding.
- The 96 pct "no interest rate" is as filed: the page prints the margin and floor only, no coupon; nothing
  but the margin, floor and (where present) PIK is tagged on floating loans.
- Not reported by any totals check: money-market rows missing from 2025-06-30; positions the page carries at a
  dash fair value are not published (9 to 24 rows, 7M to 26M of cost a quarter); issuer_name is the dumped
  member (industry + group + entity + lien words); net assets missing in 10 of 13 quarters; borrowings at
  2023-12-31 is the 2024-12-31 figure.

### 2a. Bare "Warrants" member = the category total, published as a position (REAL, GENERIC)

9 quarters, 2023-03-31 to 2025-03-31: 461, 337, 371, 199, 258, 170, 134, 136, 215 (and the row's cost 389 to 965).
Member is exactly "Warrants" (`investmentidentifieraxis=Warrants`); the same value is the tagged category
"Non-Controlled/Non-Affiliated Investments, Warrants" (258 at 2024-03-31) and the sum of the warrant
positions (Ambrosia 70 + Partner Therapeutics 188 = 258). It is also the only row of the fund with a percent
of net assets (0.01 to 0.05): the cause of A07 x9, F22 x9, F23.
The wrapper's `category_marker_re` lists "Preferred Equity", "Common Equity/Interests", "Structured Products and
Other", "(First|Second) Lien - Secured Debt" but not "Warrants" or "Unsecured Debt". The promoted rule
`exclude_group_name_subtotal_rows` cannot catch it: its predicate exempts any member containing "warrant".
Fix: add `Warrants` and `Unsecured Debt` (whole-member match) to the wrapper's `category_marker_re` and
`aggregate_markers`. ("Unsecured Debt" 3,560 / 3,502 / 3,409 at 2024-09-30 to 2025-03-31 is already not published.)

## 6. Promoted rules (data/overrides/agent_investigate_rules/1278752/)

- `1278752_exclude_relationship_axis_duplicates` (all quarters; members starting "Controlled Investments" /
  "Affiliated Investments"; 162 rows, 4.49bn). Correct in effect: these are the rows of the affiliate /
  controlled roll-forward table (13 to 19 with a value each quarter, 317M to 432M) and the totals tie without
  them. Its evidence text calls them axis duplicates; they are another note's rows. Not quarter-pinned. Should
  be a wrapper prefix setting (members "Affiliated Investments <name>" / "Controlled Investments <name>" with no
  comma after "Investments" = roll-forward rows), not a promoted SQL rule.
- `exclude_group_name_subtotal_rows` (all quarters; 22 rows, 343M). Correct for what it removes. Gaps: it
  exempts "Warrants" (2a); it cannot see a subtotal tagged on a full position member (2c). Its
  `measured_impact` records only 2024-09-30. Should be a wrapper setting: a member made of industry + group
  name only is a group row.
- No B2 corrections exist for this fund.
